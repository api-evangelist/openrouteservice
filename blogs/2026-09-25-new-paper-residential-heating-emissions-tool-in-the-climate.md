A new paper presents an open-source tool that estimates direct CO₂ emissions from residential heating in Germany, with results mapped at a 100-meter scale. The tool is part of HeiGIT’s Climate Action Navigator and relies on publicly available data to help with detailed spatial analysis and targeted climate action.
