​​What makes a place walkable goes beyond ​​whether​​ a footpath exists. ​​In collaboration with Walk21, HeiGIT has expanded its walkability assessment tool hiWalk with new indicators ​that capture some of wider conditions shaping people’s everyday walking experience, including​ shade, lighting, ​​and accessibility.
