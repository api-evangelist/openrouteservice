We combined Earth observation, an innovative custom-built deep-learning model, and spatial planning to map the condition of the rangelands around Töv province and Ulaanbaatar. The resulting maps provide actionable insights into where restoration should be prioritized, and where responsible solar, water harvesting, and roadside blue-green infrastructure can restore land while improving energy, water, and livelihood security.
