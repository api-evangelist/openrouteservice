Each November, the #30DayMapChallenge invites people worldwide to create and share maps, with a new theme for every day of the month. Started in 2019 by Finnish geographer and cartographer Topi Tjukanov, the challenge welcomes everyone, no matter which tools, data, or technologies they use.
