Deep Time Walk offers facilitated walks of 4.6 kilometers, where each meter represents one million years of our Earth's history. To empower their global network of facilitators, their new Route Creator tool makes planning these experiences accurate and grounded in real terrain, with the software making use of openrouteservice for snap-to-path routing, elevation data, and real-time distance calculation.
