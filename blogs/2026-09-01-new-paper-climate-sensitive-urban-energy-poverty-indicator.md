New paper published in Communications Sustainability, editorially selective open-access journal from the Nature Portfolio, presents the Climate-Sensitive Urban Energy Poverty Indicator Framework, which assesses household energy poverty under climate-sensitive urban conditions using household surveys, indoor environmental monitoring, and urban climate modeling in Temuco, Chile.
